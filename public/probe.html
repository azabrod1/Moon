If the browser stops cooperating

Safari sometimes shows “A problem repeatedly occurred” instead of reloading the page by itself. Reload it by hand — the numbers are already saved, and the page reports them the moment it opens. If Safari keeps showing that screen, open the page in a new tab; the storage is the same. Two or three runs are worth doing: take the lowest. If two runs disagree by more than one step, something else on the device was busy — try again when it is idle.
